  enabling a Google API, or any push to main.

THE RULE THAT DRIVES THE REDESIGN (from the challenge card)
  "Not a dashboard, and not a single query with a chat box on it. An agent that an intake
   worker can hold a working conversation with."
  The current right-hand panel (KPI stat tiles, 4 tabs of cards, pass/fail counters) reads as a
  dashboard. After M16 there must be NO stat tiles, NO tab bar, and NO hardcoded counters.
  Evidence lives inside the conversation; the only other surface is the brief, which the agent
  drafts as the conversation goes.

    calling open_case, pass `hazards` as short ENGLISH phrases (e.g. "no heat", "mold around

  - Keep replies under ~120 words unless Dev asks for the brief.
